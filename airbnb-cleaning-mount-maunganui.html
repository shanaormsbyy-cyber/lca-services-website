Mount Maunganui Airbnb Cleaning FAQs

Can you handle same-day turnovers at the Mount?

Yes. We specialise in same-day turnovers — completing a full clean between a morning check-out and afternoon check-in. This is especially important during peak summer season when guests are back-to-back.

Do you deal with sand and beach mess?

Yes. Sand is part of life at the Mount. We thoroughly vacuum and mop all floors, clean outdoor showers and rinse areas, and ensure no beach residue is left for incoming guests.

Do you provide linen and towel changing?

Yes. We strip and remake beds with fresh linen, replace towels, and can arrange linen laundering. We also restock toiletries and essentials if you supply them.

Can you manage key access without me being present?

Yes. We work with lockboxes, door codes, and key drops. Many of our Mount Maunganui clients manage their properties remotely — we handle all access coordination.

Do you send a report after each clean?

Yes. We send a photo report showing the property is guest-ready after every turnover so you can check in remotely before the next guest arrives.
