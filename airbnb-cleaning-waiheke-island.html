Airbnb Cleaning Waiheke Island — FAQs

Do you clean Airbnbs across all of Waiheke Island?

Yes. We cover Oneroa, Palm Beach, Ostend, Onetangi, Rocky Bay, Surfdale, and other Waiheke locations. Travel surcharges may apply to remote properties.

How do you handle tight turnovers on Waiheke?

We plan around ferry schedules and coordinate with your check-in/check-out times. We can usually complete same-day turnovers even in peak season.

Can you manage the property between my visits?

Yes. We offer a property management cleaning service for Waiheke holiday homes, including regular cleans, linen management, and damage reporting.

Do you clean outdoor areas and decks on Waiheke?

Yes. Deck cleaning, outdoor furniture wipe-downs, and BBQ cleaning are included or available as add-ons for Waiheke properties.
