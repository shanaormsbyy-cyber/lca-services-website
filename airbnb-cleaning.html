Common Questions

Airbnb Cleaning FAQs

Do you clean to Airbnb's guest standards?

Yes. We specialise in Airbnb and Bookabach short-stay property turnovers. Our cleans are completed to five-star guest expectations — freshly made beds, spotless bathrooms, fully reset kitchens, and restocked amenities every time.

Do you offer same-day Airbnb turnovers in Auckland?

In many cases, yes. We have same-day availability for Airbnb turnovers across Auckland, Waiheke Island, and other service areas. Contact us as early as possible — ideally as soon as your guest checks out — and we will do our best to accommodate.

Do you service bach and holiday home cleaning on Waiheke Island?

Yes. Waiheke Island bach and holiday home cleaning is one of our specialities. We coordinate around ferry schedules and guest check-in times to ensure your island property is always guest-ready.

Do you offer linen hire and restocking services?

Yes. We offer hotel-fresh linen hire — sheets and towels laundered and presented for every guest. Ask about this when you request your quote and we will include it in your cleaning package.

How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Auckland?

Prices start from $89 for a 1–2 bedroom property, $149 for 3 bedrooms, and $199+ for 4+ bedrooms. Larger properties, Waiheke Island baches, and deep cleans are quoted individually. Contact us for a free no-obligation quote.

What areas do you cover for Airbnb cleaning?

We cover Auckland and surrounds (including Waiheke Island), Hamilton, Waikato, Tauranga, and Waihi. Use our postcode checker on the Service Areas page or call to confirm your location.
