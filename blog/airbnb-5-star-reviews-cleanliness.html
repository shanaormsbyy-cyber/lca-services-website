How to Get Consistent 5-Star Reviews Through Cleanliness

Cleanliness is the single biggest driver of Airbnb guest reviews. Learn how professional turnover cleans protect your rating — and your income.

📅 March 2025 ⏱ 5 min read ✍ LCA Cleaning Team
← Back to Blog

If you're an Airbnb host in New Zealand — whether you're listing a bach in Waihi, a studio in Auckland, or a holiday home on Waiheke Island — you already know that cleanliness is the single biggest driver of guest reviews. Guests may forgive a slow Wi-Fi connection or a dated couch, but they will not forgive a dirty bathroom or stained sheets. And on Airbnb's platform, where your overall star rating determines your visibility in search results, a single 3-star review for cleanliness can sink your ranking for weeks. This guide breaks down exactly how professional cleaning standards lead to consistent 5-star reviews, and why so many NZ hosts are turning to professional turnaround cleaning services to protect their income.

Why Cleanliness Is the Highest-Stakes Airbnb Rating Category

Airbnb guests rate their stay across six categories: overall experience, cleanliness, accuracy, check-in, communication, and value. Of these, cleanliness has the most immediate emotional impact. It's the first thing a guest physically encounters when they walk through the door. Before they've tested the bed, connected to the Wi-Fi, or checked how good the view is — they've already assessed whether the space feels clean.

Research consistently shows that cleanliness complaints are the number one reason guests leave negative reviews on short-term rental platforms. In the New Zealand context, where Airbnb hosting has grown sharply across Auckland, the Waikato, Tauranga, and the Bay of Plenty, competition between listings is intense. A property with a 4.7 cleanliness rating competes poorly against one sitting at 4.95.

The Airbnb Algorithm Rewards Cleanliness

Airbnb's search ranking algorithm gives significant weight to review scores. Hosts with consistently high cleanliness ratings are more likely to be awarded Superhost status, which requires an overall rating of 4.8 or above. Superhosts receive a badge, increased search visibility, and priority customer support — all of which translate directly to more bookings and higher nightly rates.

The practical implication: cleanliness isn't just about guest satisfaction. It's a revenue driver.

What Guests Actually Notice (That Hosts Often Miss)

Most hosts clean their properties with good intentions but without the systematic rigour of a professional. Here are the areas guests notice most — and that are most commonly missed in a rushed DIY clean.

The Bathroom Is Make or Break

Guests assess bathroom cleanliness within seconds of entering. Soap scum on the shower screen, limescale around the taps, mould in the grout, and a toilet that hasn't been properly sanitised under the rim are all instant review-killers. New Zealand's humid climate — particularly in Auckland and the Waikato — accelerates mould growth and limescale build-up, making regular deep cleaning of bathrooms non-negotiable.

Bedding and Linen Need Hotel-Level Presentation

Crumpled duvet covers, pillowcases with faint staining, or linen that smells musty will generate a cleanliness complaint every single time. Fresh, crisp, hotel-folded linen signals quality and care before a guest has said a word. Invest in at least two full sets of linen per bed so turnaround cleaning doesn't depend on everything being washed and dried in time.

Kitchen Odours and Surfaces

Even if your kitchen looks clean, odours from a poorly cleaned oven, a grimy rangehood filter, or a fridge that hasn't been wiped down tell a different story. Guests who arrive to cook a meal and find the oven coated in old grease will photograph it and mention it in their review.

The "Touch Test" Areas

Professional cleaners talk about the "touch test" — the surfaces guests will instinctively touch and examine. Light switches, door handles, remote controls, and window ledges accumulate grime quickly. A guest running a finger across a dusty windowsill or picking up a sticky remote control will notice.

How Professional Turnaround Cleans Protect Your Rating

The key difference between a DIY host clean and a professional turnaround clean is consistency and time. A professional cleaning team follows a structured checklist, every time, regardless of how trashed the previous guests left the place. They're not cutting corners because they're tired or running late for something else.

A Standardised Checklist Eliminates Variability

Professional Airbnb cleaning services in New Zealand work from room-by-room checklists that cover every surface, fixture, and fitting. This means the 8th clean of the month is as thorough as the first. For hosts managing multiple properties — increasingly common in Auckland and Tauranga — this consistency is impossible to achieve without professional support.

Same-Day Turnarounds with Tight Check-In/Check-Out Windows

Many NZ Airbnb hosts operate with a 10am checkout and 3pm check-in. That's five hours to strip linen, launder, clean every room, restock supplies, and reset the property presentation. Professional cleaners experienced in short-term rental turnarounds know how to work efficiently within that window without compromising quality.

Post-Clean Inspection and Reporting

Reputable Airbnb cleaning services will conduct a post-clean inspection and flag any damage, missing items, or maintenance issues — things a solo host cleaning between calls and school pickups is far more likely to miss.

Setting Up a 5-Star System: Practical Steps for NZ Hosts

Beyond the clean itself, here are the systems that separate consistently 5-star properties from those that hover around 4.6.

Stock a proper welcome kit. Fresh soap, shampoo, conditioner, hand lotion, toilet paper in excess, and a clean welcome note signal host attentiveness. Guests notice when supplies run out mid-stay.

Communicate proactively about cleanliness. A short message before check-in saying "the property has just been professionally cleaned and is ready for you" sets a positive expectation and frames the experience before they walk in the door.

Act immediately on any cleanliness feedback. If a guest mentions something in a review, address it before the next booking. Repeat complaints kill your rating faster than a single bad review.

Schedule deep cleans quarterly. On top of your turnaround cleans, a quarterly deep clean — including oven interiors, rangehood filters, window tracks, under furniture, and grouting — maintains the property at a standard that casual turnaround cleaning can't sustain long-term.
