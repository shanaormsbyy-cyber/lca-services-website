The Airbnb Hosting Checklist Every New Host Needs

From your first listing to your first five-star review — the practical checklist built from working with Airbnb hosts across New Zealand.

📅 December 2024 ⏱ 6 min read ✍ LCA Cleaning Team
← Back to Blog

So you've listed your first property on Airbnb. You've sorted the photos, set your nightly rate, written your listing description, and connected your calendar. Now comes the part that most new hosts underestimate: the operational reality of hosting. Getting your first booking is exciting. Getting your fifth, tenth, and fiftieth — consistently, with great reviews and repeat guests — requires systems. This checklist covers everything a new Airbnb host in New Zealand needs to set up, manage, and maintain a short-term rental that earns strong reviews and sustainable income. Whether you're hosting in Auckland, on Waiheke Island, in Tauranga, or in the Waikato, these fundamentals apply.

Before Your First Guest: The Setup Checklist

Getting the property set up correctly before your first guest is far more effective than fixing problems reactively after a negative review. Take the time to do this properly once and you'll save yourself significant stress later. For ongoing turnover cleaning, our Airbnb cleaning service in Auckland and across NZ keeps every guest arrival guest-ready.

Safety and Compliance

  • Smoke alarms: Under New Zealand's Residential Tenancies (Smoke Alarms and Insulation) Regulations, working smoke alarms are legally required in rental properties. While Airbnb properties operate under different legal frameworks than long-term rentals, smoke alarm compliance is non-negotiable for guest safety and your legal protection.
  • Carbon monoxide alarm: Required if the property has a gas appliance or internal garage.
  • First aid kit: A basic kit should be accessible and stocked.
  • Emergency contact list: Post this visibly in the property — local emergency services (111 in New Zealand), your contact number, and the address of the property written out (guests may not know the address in an emergency).
  • Fire extinguisher and fire blanket: Particularly important in the kitchen.

Documentation for Guests

  • House manual: A clearly written manual covering how to operate appliances (heat pump, oven, dishwasher, TV, WiFi), rubbish and recycling collection days and procedures, parking instructions, and any property-specific quirks. The house manual reduces guest messages dramatically.
  • Check-in instructions: Clear, step-by-step instructions sent to guests 24 hours before arrival. Do not assume the listing description is sufficient.
  • House rules: Clearly communicated in the listing and reinforced in a welcome note in the property.

The Welcome Experience

  • Welcome note: A brief, personal handwritten or printed note creates warmth and sets a positive tone. It costs nothing and guests mention it in reviews.
  • Local recommendations: A short list of your favourite local restaurants, cafés, beaches, and attractions. For Waiheke Island or Waihi hosts, this is particularly valued by guests unfamiliar with the area.
  • Welcome supplies: A small supply of tea, coffee, milk, and a few snacks is a consistently appreciated touch that generates "host goes above and beyond" mentions in reviews.

The Cleaning and Presentation Checklist

This is the most operationally critical area for maintaining your Airbnb rating. Cleanliness reviews on Airbnb are unforgiving — one bad clean can generate a 3-star review that suppresses your ranking for months.

Linen and Towels

  • At least two full sets of linen per bed — one on, one in the wash
  • Hotel-style duvet folding and presentation
  • Fresh, clean, odour-free towels folded and displayed attractively
  • Clean bath mat for every guest
  • No staining, pilling, or discolouration — replace worn items promptly

The Cleaning Standard by Room

Kitchen: - Oven cleaned (inside and out, including racks) - Rangehood and filter - All appliances wiped down — microwave interior and exterior, kettle, toaster, coffee maker - Fridge wiped out, expired condiments removed - Bench tops and splashback sanitised - Sink cleaned and polished - All dishes and utensils clean and correctly stored - Bin emptied and relined

Bathrooms: - Toilet cleaned inside (including under the rim) and out - Basin and taps polished - Shower or bath cleaned and de-scaled - Mirror cleaned streak-free - Grout visibly clean (no mould) - Fresh towels and restock of soap, shampoo, conditioner, toilet paper

Bedrooms: - Fresh linen — hotel-standard presentation - Wardrobes and drawers cleared of previous guests' items - Under-bed area vacuumed - Surfaces dusted - Light fittings clear of insects and dust

Living Areas: - All surfaces dusted - Vacuuming or mopping - Remote controls wiped - Cushions and throws straightened - Windows and glass doors cleaned

The Restock Checklist

Keep a running stock of consumables so you're never caught short: - Toilet paper (stock at least 2 rolls per bathroom per guest per night) - Hand soap and dish soap - Washing machine detergent - Coffee, tea, sugar, milk (if you offer these) - Rubbish bags - Paper towels or kitchen rolls - Dishwasher tablets if applicable

Between-Guest Operations Checklist

Immediately After Checkout

  • Inspect the property room by room — photograph any damage before cleaning
  • Strip all linen and towels immediately
  • Note any maintenance issues — a burnt-out light bulb, a sticking lock, a dripping tap — and address them before the next guest

During the Turnaround Clean

  • Follow your full cleaning checklist (or brief your professional cleaner on your expectations)
  • Check all supplies are restocked
  • Reset all presentation elements — cushions, throw, welcome note, welcome supplies
  • Test all appliances and technology — heat pump remote, TV, WiFi, oven

Before Every Check-In

  • Confirm the property is ready at least 2 hours before check-in time
  • Send check-in instructions and a welcome message to the arriving guest
  • Ensure access arrangements (key safe code, smart lock access) are working

Managing Your Airbnb Rating Long-Term

Respond to reviews promptly and professionally. Even critical reviews, responded to calmly and constructively, demonstrate host professionalism to future guests.

Proactively request reviews. After checkout, a brief, friendly message thanking the guest and mentioning that reviews are helpful is a simple tactic that significantly increases review rates.

Act on feedback immediately. If a guest mentions something in a message or review, fix it before the next booking. Repeated complaints about the same issue are avoidable.

Schedule quarterly deep cleans. Regular turnaround cleans maintain the surface presentation, but quarterly deep cleans — covering oven internals, rangehood filters, grout, window tracks, and under-furniture areas — maintain the property at a standard that casual cleaning can't sustain over time.

Working with a Professional Airbnb Cleaner

Many successful Airbnb hosts in New Zealand — particularly those managing multiple properties or working full-time alongside their hosting — use professional short-term rental cleaning services. The benefits are significant: consistent quality across every clean, ability to handle same-day turnarounds, reliable cover when your schedule doesn't allow for a DIY clean, and post-clean inspection reports that flag damage or maintenance issues.

When choosing a professional Airbnb cleaning service in New Zealand, look for a company that: - Has specific experience with short-term rental turnarounds (not just domestic cleaning) - Works from a detailed, room-by-room checklist - Communicates proactively about any issues found - Can be relied on for short-notice bookings
