Post-Construction Cleaning Across Auckland

Auckland is booming with new builds, renovations, and commercial fit-outs. Our builders clean team operates across all Auckland regions — North Shore, West Auckland, South Auckland, and the CBD. We coordinate with builders, project managers, and developers to fit your timeline.

We follow a 3-phase process: rough clean (construction waste removal), detail clean (surfaces, joinery, windows), and final sparkle (handover-ready polish). Each phase is documented with photos.

Builders Clean Auckland — FAQs

Do you work directly with builders and developers?

Yes. We regularly work alongside Auckland builders, project managers, and developers. We can be added to your build schedule and coordinate with your site manager.

What areas of Auckland do you cover?

All of Auckland — North Shore, West Auckland, South Auckland, Central Auckland, and Eastern Suburbs. We also cover Pukekohe and Warkworth for larger projects.

How long does an Auckland builders clean take?

A standard 3-bedroom new build typically takes 6–8 hours for a full builders clean. Commercial and larger residential projects are assessed individually.

Do you clean commercial construction sites in Auckland?

Yes. We handle commercial builders cleans for office fit-outs, retail spaces, hospitality venues, and industrial buildings across Auckland.
