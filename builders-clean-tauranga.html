Tauranga

Post-Construction Cleaning Across Tauranga & Bay of Plenty

Tauranga is one of New Zealand's fastest-growing cities and its construction sector reflects that. New subdivisions in Papamoa, The Lakes, and Bethlehem are being completed rapidly alongside commercial developments at Tauriko Business Estate. Our Tauranga builders clean team slots into your build schedule and delivers a thorough, documented clean at every phase.

We follow a proven 3-phase process: rough clean (debris, dust, construction waste), detail clean (surfaces, frames, joinery, glass), and final sparkle (full handover-ready polish). We photograph every stage.
