Common Questions

Builders Clean FAQs

What is a builders clean?

A builders clean is a specialist post-construction clean carried out after building work, renovations, or fit-outs are complete. It removes construction dust, plaster residue, paint marks, silicone smears, and building debris to prepare the space for handover or occupation.

What are the stages of a builders clean?

A full builders clean typically involves three stages: a rough clean (removing rubble and large debris), a detailed clean (cleaning surfaces, windows, and fixtures), and a final sparkle clean (polishing and final inspection-ready presentation). LCA Services can carry out all three stages or just the final clean — depending on your needs.

Do you clean new builds and renovations across Auckland?

Yes. We provide builders cleans for new residential builds, renovations, extensions, and commercial fit-outs across Auckland, Waiheke Island, Hamilton, Waikato, Tauranga, and Waihi.

How much does a builders clean cost in NZ?

Builders clean pricing starts from $299 for smaller properties (1–2 bedrooms), $449 for 3 bedrooms, and $599+ for larger homes and commercial spaces. Final pricing depends on the size of the project, the amount of construction residue, and whether multiple stages are required.

How soon after construction can you clean?

We can usually schedule within a few days of your project completion. We recommend booking in advance so we can align with your handover timeline. Contact us on to arrange a site visit and quote.

Do you provide builders cleans for commercial fit-outs and offices?

Yes. We provide post-construction and fit-out cleaning for commercial spaces including offices, retail fit-outs, restaurants, and industrial buildings across Auckland and the wider NZ service area.
