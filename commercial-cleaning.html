Common Questions

Commercial Cleaning FAQs

What types of businesses do you clean?

We clean offices, retail stores, warehouses, medical and dental practices, gyms, cafés and restaurants, childcare centres, and other commercial premises across Auckland, Hamilton, and Tauranga.

Do you offer after-hours and weekend commercial cleaning?

Yes. We offer flexible scheduling including early morning, evening, and weekend cleans to minimise disruption to your business. We work around your operating hours.

Are your commercial cleaners police vetted and insured?

Yes. All LCA Services team members are police vetted and our business is fully insured. We take the security and safety of your premises seriously.

Can I get a regular commercial cleaning contract?

Yes. We offer daily, weekly, fortnightly, and monthly commercial cleaning contracts. Regular contract customers receive priority scheduling and consistent team allocation.

Do you supply all cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We arrive fully equipped with professional-grade cleaning products, vacuums, mop heads, and microfibre cloths. Cloths are laundered after every job and never cross-contaminated between premises.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Auckland?

Commercial cleaning is priced based on the size of your premises, frequency of cleaning, and scope of work. We offer competitive contract rates and will provide a free, no-obligation quote after a walkthrough of your space.
