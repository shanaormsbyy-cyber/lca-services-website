Bond Cleaning Across Auckland's North Shore

The North Shore is Auckland's largest rental sub-market — with thousands of active tenancies in suburbs like Takapuna, Albany, Milford, Glenfield, and Devonport. We clean to the inspection standards of major North Shore property managers including Bayleys North Shore, Barfoot & Thompson Takapuna, Harcourts North Shore, Professionals Albany, and Ray White North Shore.

Our team brings all professional-grade supplies and equipment. We photograph the completed clean and can email documentation directly to your property manager.

For Devonport and Bayswater properties, we factor in access logistics. For apartments in Takapuna and high-rises in Albany, we are experienced with key safes, intercom systems, and building management access.

Which North Shore property managers do you work with?
