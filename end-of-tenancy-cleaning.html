Common Questions

Bond Clean FAQs

What is a bond clean in New Zealand?

A bond clean (also called an end of tenancy clean or vacate clean) is a thorough clean of a rental property at the end of a tenancy. It must meet the standard required by the landlord or property manager to release your bond under the Residential Tenancies Act.

What is included in your end of tenancy clean?

Our end of tenancy clean covers every room including all kitchen surfaces, appliances, and inside the oven; bathroom and toilet scrubbing and disinfection; window sills and tracks; skirting boards; carpet vacuuming; and mopping of all hard floors. We clean to the standard expected by NZ property managers.

Do you guarantee I will get my bond back?

Our bond cleans are completed to the standard required by NZ landlords and property managers. If anything is missed on inspection, we offer a free re-clean within 72 hours. We work with your property manager's checklist to ensure nothing is overlooked.

How much does end of tenancy cleaning cost in New Zealand?

The cost of a bond clean depends on the size and condition of the property. Most bond cleans start from $450 + GST, but every job is different. We offer a free quote for your property so you get an exact price with no surprises — contact us on 022 487 1791 or fill in our online form to get started.

How far in advance should I book my bond clean?

We recommend booking at least 3–5 days before your tenancy end date to ensure availability. We do accept last-minute bookings subject to availability — contact us and we will do our best to fit you in.

Do you cover end of tenancy cleaning across Waikato and Tauranga?

Yes. We provide end of tenancy and bond cleans across Auckland, Waiheke Island, Hamilton and the Waikato region, Tauranga, and Waihi. Contact us to confirm availability for your area.
