End of Tenancy Cleaning
Our end of tenancy cleans are designed specifically to meet the standard expected by landlords and property managers under the NZ Residential Tenancies Act. We cover all the areas that typically cause bond deductions — oven, rangehood, bathroom grout, skirting boards, windows, and more.
Standard carpet vacuuming is included in every end of tenancy clean. Professional carpet steam cleaning is available as an add-on — ask us about this when requesting your quote.
Yes — full oven deep cleaning (interior, racks, trays, and glass) is included as standard in our end of tenancy clean. The oven is one of the most common areas flagged during exit inspections, so we make sure it's done thoroughly every time.
We recommend booking at least 1–2 weeks before your move-out date to secure your preferred time. We do have last-minute availability in some cases — contact us and we'll see what we can do.
We cover basic outdoor tasks including sweeping paths, wiping outdoor furniture, and general tidying. Lawn mowing is available as an add-on service — let us know when you request your quote.
Builders Clean
A rough clean covers large debris removal, bulk sweeping, and clearing construction waste. The main clean deep scrubs all surfaces, floors, and fixtures. The final clean is a detail pass — fine dust removal, streak-free glass polishing, and a full walk-through before handover. We offer all three phases.
Yes — paint splatter and overspray removal from glass is included in our builders clean. We use appropriate scrapers and techniques to remove splatter without scratching the glass surface.
Yes. We work with builders, developers, and homeowners on projects of all sizes — from single homes to multi-unit developments. Contact us with details of your project and we'll put together a tailored quote.
Yes — exterior pressure washing of paths, driveways, patios, and external walls is available as part of our builders clean service. Let us know when requesting your quote.
