Questions

Property Manager FAQs

Can you liaise directly with tenants or manage access?

Yes. We can coordinate directly with tenants for access, collect keys from key safes, or work with property access codes. Many PMs provide us the access details and we handle the rest.

What happens if a property manager's inspection fails?

We offer a 48-hour re-clean guarantee. If the property manager identifies a cleaning issue within 48 hours of our clean, we return to fix it at no cost to you or your client.

Do you offer discounts for property management companies?

Yes. Property managers placing regular bookings receive discounted rates. Contact us to discuss your typical monthly volume and we'll put together a partnership pricing structure.

How quickly can you turn around a bond clean in Auckland?

For property management partners we offer priority scheduling. In most cases we can complete a bond clean within 2–3 business days of the vacate date — or sooner if you need it urgently.

Do you cover all three cities — Auckland, Hamilton, and Tauranga?

Yes. LCA Services covers Auckland (including North Shore), Hamilton and wider Waikato, and Tauranga and Bay of Plenty. Multi-city property managers can use a single contact for all three regions.
